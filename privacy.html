Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who uses the app. Only the owner of the TikTok account @gta6streamer.moments. The app does not collect data from any other person.

What the app accesses. After the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token that allows it to publish videos to the owner's account (permission "video.publish"). It also reads the owner's account display name and the allowed privacy options at posting time.

Where data is kept. The tokens are stored only in a private file on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server and not shared with anyone.

Sharing and selling. The app does not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties, and does not use any data for advertising.

Retention and deletion. The owner can delete the token file at any time, or revoke the app in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

Contact. Send a message to @gta6streamer.moments on TikTok.
